Overview

Shane practices with the Business + Transactions, Taxation and Commercial Finance teams, advising on securities law, business law, commercial lending, contracts and public finance.

He brings more than 15 years of experience, including prior practice at Paul, Weiss, Rifkind, Wharton & Garrison LLP and at Burak Anderson & Melloni, PLC in Vermont, handling complex corporate matters with a particular focus on securities laws for local and national clients.

His practice includes representing entrepreneurs and start-ups in seed, venture capital and private equity financings and corporate governance — from business formation through operating, limited partnership, shareholders, securities purchase and registration rights agreements.

On the investor side, Shane has represented private equity investors, hedge funds and institutional investors in commercial finance transactions involving privately held and publicly traded companies.

Shane grew up in Charlotte and Jericho, Vermont, and now lives with his family in Underhill.
